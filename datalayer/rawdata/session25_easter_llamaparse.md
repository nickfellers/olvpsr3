## 3-Minute Retreat
As you prepare the session, pause, be still, and be aware of the loving presence of God.

> **Ephesians 2:4–7**
> *God, who is rich in mercy . . . brought us to life with Christ . . . , raised us up with him, and seated us with him in the heavens . . . that in the ages to come he might show the immeasurable riches of his grace in his kindness to us in Christ Jesus.*

### Reflection
We do not earn God’s mercy through our actions. When Paul says that God is rich in mercy, he means that God has more mercy to give than will ever be needed. Since we are God’s creation, we are the recipients of that mercy, poured out in Jesus, God’s only Son. In Paul’s letter to the Ephesians, we see that God’s mercy comes to us in Jesus. God loves us completely, and there is no end to the grace he bestows on us. God’s mercy is greater than anything we do or desire.

What do I need to ask of Jesus as I plan for my catechesis and encounter the children I teach?

## Knowing and Sharing Your Faith in Session 25
Consider how Scripture and Tradition can deepen your understanding of the session content.

### Scripture
**Luke 6:35–36** tells us that Jesus teaches us to give without expecting anything in return.

**Psalm 103:8** teaches us that God is unendingly merciful.

**Ephesians 2:4–7** says that God’s mercy, grace, and kindness are limitless.

### Tradition
Easter Sunday begins a 50-day celebration of the Paschal Mystery—the Passion, Death, Resurrection, and Ascension of Jesus Christ. Easter Sunday is the third and final day of the Paschal Triduum, the three days beginning on the evening of Holy Thursday. On Holy Saturday evening, we celebrate the Easter Vigil; a fire, and from it the paschal candle, are lit. The story of salvation is told, the baptismal waters are blessed, and new converts are received into the Church. Through his Resurrection, Christ gives us new birth in the Holy Spirit and renews the gift of life within us.

**Read and listen to Luke 6:35–36.**

Jesus asks us to give and not expect anything in return, to be kind whether or not the person we are kind to is good. This is what God the Father does. God did not send us Jesus in return for our being good. He sent Jesus to show that he loves us as we are. We rejoice in God's merciful love during the Easter season. Easter Sunday is the most important day of the Church year. It is celebrated on the first Sunday after the first full moon of spring. It begins with the Easter Vigil the night before, Holy Saturday. During the Easter season, we also celebrate the Ascension of Jesus into heaven and Pentecost, the last day of this sacred season.

### UNDERSTANDING SCRIPTURE
During his time on earth, Jesus preached that we should forgive others, even our enemies, just as God offers us his forgiveness. Jesus' Death was the supreme act of love and forgiveness. He suffered that Death in order to be resurrected. His love and forgiveness are renewed forever through his Resurrection. In contemplating God's mercy at Easter, we are invited to consider how Christ's forgiveness can inspire us to show mercy to those who have caused harm, even when it feels unreasonable or perhaps even impossible to do so.

# Mass During Easter
When you go to Mass during Easter, pay special attention to the prayer to the **Lamb of God** during the Fraction Rite. Jesus, the Lamb of God, gave up his life for the salvation of the world. Before receiving Holy Communion, we pray to the Lamb of God to have mercy on us.

## What We Experience
When you look around your church at Easter, you may see images of the Lamb of God. This reminds us of Jesus' great sacrifice. The parish sings together joyfully that Jesus is with us always. White is the liturgical color of the season. It represents the joy of Christ's Resurrection. The priest wears white or gold vestments. The altar cloth is white. White lilies symbolize our new life in the risen Christ.

> ### Did You Know?
> The Ascension is celebrated on the seventh Sunday of Easter.

## A Celebration of Life
Easter is the celebration of life. Jesus was born and lived to teach us how to be closer to God his Father. Jesus died so that our sins could be forgiven and our souls could be joined forever with God. God sent his Son, Jesus, to show us his mercy and love.

## The Resurrection
After Jesus' Death, his friends came to his tomb and found it empty. His friends were afraid. Jesus appeared to Mary Magdalene and told her not to be sad. He also appeared many times to comfort and encourage the Apostles. He appeared to Peter with the instructions to take care of his sheep. By "sheep," he meant his people. As disciples we are called to do the same as Peter. We celebrate Easter by committing ourselves to the service of others.

#### Retell the Story
Arrange children into four groups, one for each Gospel. Give each group a Bible and assign it the task of researching the Resurrection account from its assigned Gospel.
