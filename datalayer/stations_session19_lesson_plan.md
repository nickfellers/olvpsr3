# Grade 3 Religious Education
## Composite Lesson: Stations of the Cross + Session 19
**Theme:** Called to Respond — Jesus gave everything; we answer with our gifts and our vocation
**Total Time:** 60 Minutes
**Date Taught:** March 8, 2026

---

## Thematic Thread

Jesus gave everything for us through his Passion and Death (Stations of the Cross) → His sacrifice calls for a response → Our vocation is how we answer that call → God equips us with gifts and talents to serve others. **"God gave you gifts — use them to serve."**

## OPENING (2 min) — 0:00–0:02

- Teams sit together. Bibles out on desks.
- Quick outline: "We're in Lent right now — the season where we prepare for Easter by remembering what Jesus did for us. Today we're going to walk through the Stations of the Cross — the story of Jesus' last hours. Then we're going to ask: if Jesus gave everything for us, **how do we respond?**"

---

## SEGMENT 1: Stations of the Cross (13 min) — 0:02–0:15

### Introduction (1 min)

**Teacher says:**
- "The 14 Stations of the Cross tell the story of Jesus' Passion and Death."
- "Churches have these on their walls — you may have seen them at OLV. At each station, we stop and think about what Jesus went through — for us."
- "We're going to walk through six of the most important stations together."

### Six Key Stations (~2 min each)

**Station 1 — Jesus Is Condemned to Death**
- "Pontius Pilate condemns Jesus to death — even though Jesus did nothing wrong. Jesus accepted it. He didn't argue. He didn't fight."
- Reflection: "Have you ever been blamed for something you didn't do? Jesus knows what that feels like."

**Station 4 — Jesus Meets His Sorrowful Mother**
- "On his way to the cross, Jesus sees his mother, Mary. Her heart is breaking. But she stays. She doesn't leave."
- Reflection: "Mary couldn't stop what was happening — but she showed up. Sometimes just being there for someone is the most important thing."

**Station 5 — Simon of Cyrene Helps Jesus Carry the Cross**
- "Soldiers grab a man named Simon from the crowd and force him to help carry the cross. Simon didn't volunteer. But he helped anyway."
- Reflection: "Sometimes we're called to help even when we didn't plan to. Simon answered the call — will we?"

**Station 11 — Jesus Is Nailed to the Cross**
- "This is the moment. Jesus' hands and feet are nailed to the cross. He could have stopped it — he's God — but he chose to stay. For us."
- *Pause briefly. Let it land.*

**Station 12 — Jesus Dies on the Cross**
- "After suffering greatly, Jesus bows his head and dies."
- *Pause.*
- "He gave everything. Everything."

**Station 14 — Jesus Is Laid in the Tomb**
- "His friends take his body down and place it in the tomb."
- "But we know — this isn't the end. Easter is coming."

### Quick Summary of Remaining Stations (1 min)

"We focused on six stations, but there are 14 total. The others include: Jesus takes up the cross, he falls three times under the weight, a woman named Veronica wipes his face, he meets the women of Jerusalem, and he is stripped of his garments. Every station shows us how much Jesus was willing to suffer — for love of us."

---

## BRIDGE (2 min) — 0:15–0:17

**Teacher says:**
- "Jesus gave everything — his whole life — to serve us and save us. That's the biggest act of love in history."
- "So now the question is: **What are we called to do in response?**"
- "The answer is: we use the gifts God gave us to serve others. That's what the rest of today is about."

---

## SEGMENT 2: Bible Search + "Great and First" Teaching (10 min) — 0:17–0:27

### Bible Search: Mark 10:43–45

**Instructions:** "Open your Bibles to Mark, chapter 10, verse 43."

**Read aloud (or have a student read):**
> "Whoever wishes to be great among you will be your servant; whoever wishes to be first among you will be the slave of all. For the Son of Man did not come to be served but to serve and to give his life as a ransom for many."

### Teaching

**Teacher says:**
- "We just walked through the Stations. Jesus gave his life. That's what he means here — he came to serve, not to be served."
- "The world says 'great' means powerful, famous, in charge. Jesus flips it. **Great means you serve.**"
- "First doesn't mean first in line. It means you put others first."

### Discussion

**Prompt:** "What does the world say makes someone great?"

**Possible answers:** Rich, famous, strong, popular, having lots of followers.

"Now what does Jesus say makes someone great?" *(Serving others, helping, putting others first)*

**Connect back to Stations:**
- "We just saw Jesus do exactly this. He didn't fight back. He carried the cross. He gave his life. That's what 'great' looks like to God."

**Write on board:** **"To be great, serve. To be first, put others first."**

---

## SEGMENT 3: Vocations — How We Respond (10 min) — 0:27–0:37

### Bridge

**Teacher says:**
- "So if Jesus gave everything and we're called to respond — how? God gives each of us a **vocation** — a calling, a way of life."

**Write on board:** **VOCATION = A CALL FROM GOD**

- "Vocation comes from a Latin word meaning 'to call.' God calls each of us to live a certain way — and he gives us the gifts to do it."

### Three Vocations to Know

**1. Holy Orders**
- "Some men are called to be priests or deacons. They celebrate the Sacrament of Holy Orders. They serve the whole Church."
- "Who's a priest you know?" *(Pull answers — your pastor, Fr. _____, etc.)*

**2. Matrimony**
- "Some men and women are called to be married. They agree to live in faithful love. That's the Sacrament of Matrimony."
- "Your parents or grandparents who are married — that's a vocation too."

**3. Religious Life / Other Vocations**
- "Some people serve God as brothers or sisters in religious orders. And every one of us has a calling — even right now, as kids."

### Quick Check

- "What's a vocation?" *(A call from God to live a certain way)*
- "Name one sacrament we just learned about." *(Holy Orders or Matrimony)*

### Saint Andrew Kim Taegon (brief)

**Teacher says:**
- "Saint Andrew Kim Taegon was the first Korean-born priest. He traveled to China as a teenager to study for the priesthood, then came back to Korea to share God's Word."
- "He was killed for his faith — martyred — but he never stopped answering God's call."
- "His feast day is September 20."
- "Saint Andrew answered his call no matter what it cost him. What's God calling you to do?"

---

## SEGMENT 4: Our Gifts — What God Gave You to Respond With (8 min) — 0:37–0:45

### Bridge

**Teacher says:**
- "So God calls us through a vocation. But he also equips us. He gives each of us **gifts and talents** — and they're all different."

### Reading God's Word: 1 Peter 4:10

**Read aloud:**
> "As each one has received a gift, use it to serve one another as good stewards of God's varied grace."

### Teaching

**Teacher says:**
- "God doesn't give everyone the same gifts. Some of you are great listeners. Some are funny. Some are good at sports, or art, or math, or being kind when someone is sad."
- "Every single one of those is a gift from God — and he gave it to you for a reason: **to serve others.**"

### Discussion

**Prompt:** "Name a gift or talent you have — and it doesn't have to be a school subject. Maybe you're really good at making people laugh. Maybe you're the person who notices when someone is left out."

Pull 4–5 answers. After each: "That's a gift from God. How could you use that to help someone?"

### Connect to Stations

- "Remember Simon of Cyrene? He helped carry the cross. That was his gift in that moment — his strength. He used it to serve Jesus."
- "You have gifts too. The question is: **will you use them?**"

**Add to board:** **"God gave you gifts — use them to serve."**

---

## SEGMENT 5: Activity — My Gifts in Action (8 min) — 0:45–0:53

Hand out paper (or use back of a handout).

### Part 1 — My Gift (3 min)

**Instructions:** "Draw or write one gift God gave you — something you do well or love to do."

### Part 2 — How I'll Use It (5 min)

**Instructions:** "Now draw or write how you could use that gift to serve someone this week. Be specific — who will you help? What will you do?"

**Share:** Have 2–3 volunteers share. After each: "That's answering God's call. That's your vocation right now."

---

## SEGMENT 6: Speed Round Review (5 min) — 0:53–0:58

Rapid-fire to each team. One point each.

1. How many Stations of the Cross are there? *(14)*
2. What does "vocation" mean? *(A call from God / a way of life)*
3. In Mark 10, Jesus says to be great you must ___. *(Serve)*
4. Name one of the two sacraments we learned about today. *(Holy Orders or Matrimony)*
5. Who was the first Korean-born priest? *(Saint Andrew Kim Taegon)*
6. Who helped Jesus carry the cross? *(Simon of Cyrene)*
7. Finish this: "God gave you gifts — ___." *(Use them to serve)*
8. What does 1 Peter 4:10 tell us to do with our gifts? *(Use them to serve one another)*

---

## CLOSING PRAYER (2 min) — 0:58–1:00

**Teacher says:** "Let's close with a prayer. Repeat after me."

> Lord Jesus,
> Thank you for giving everything for us.
> Thank you for the gifts you've given each of us.
> Help us hear your call.
> Help us use our gifts to serve others.
> Like Simon who carried your cross,
> help us show up when someone needs us.
> To be great, we serve.
> To be first, we put others first.
> Amen.
