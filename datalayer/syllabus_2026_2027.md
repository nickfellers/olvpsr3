## Standing instructions (from the syllabus)

- Send home the **Respond** and **At Home** page at the end of each chapter and talk about ways they can do some of the things on the pages.
- Tear out the pages to be used each week and send them home with the students each week.
- See the coordinator's weekly email for current details.
- The syllabus is suggested so that we follow the liturgical calendar and other enrichments planned for the Third Grade curriculum.
- Prayers: Morning Offering pg. 249 and Prayer to the Holy Spirit pg. 249.
- Send home workbooks at the end of the school year with the students.

- **Deacon Rob** — Baptism of the Lord talk + Blessing of the Chalk (Jan 10).
- There is a second 3rd grade class (joint pageant rehearsals).
