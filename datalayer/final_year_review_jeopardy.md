### CATEGORY 1: JESUS & THE PASCHAL MYSTERY

**$100** The name JESUS means "God _____."
- Answer: Saves

**$200** The Paschal Mystery includes Jesus' life, Death, Resurrection, and _____.
- Answer: Ascension

**$300** Easter Sunday is the most _____ day of the Church year.
- Answer: Important

**$400** How many days after Jesus died did he rise from the dead?
- Answer: 3 (Three)

**$500** _____ days after his Resurrection, Jesus ascended into heaven.
- Answer: 40

### CATEGORY 2: CARLO ACUTIS

**$100** Carlo Acutis was a boy from _____ (the country shaped like a boot).
- Answer: Italy

**$200** Carlo called the Eucharist his "_____ to heaven."
- Answer: Highway

**$300** Carlo went to _____ every single day.
- Answer: Mass

**$400** Carlo set his own _____ time limits because he didn't want anything to crowd out his love for God.
- Answer: Screen

**$500** Carlo said: "All people are born as originals, but many die as _____."
- Answer: Photocopies

### CATEGORY 3: THE HOLY SPIRIT & PENTECOST

**$100** The three Persons of the Holy Trinity are the Father, the Son, and the _____.
- Answer: Holy Spirit

**$200** The Holy Spirit came to the disciples at _____.
- Answer: Pentecost

**$300** On Pentecost Sunday, priests wear _____ vestments.
- Answer: Red

**$400** The 7 _____ of the Holy Spirit include wisdom, understanding, and fortitude.
- Answer: Gifts

**$500** Pentecost is celebrated _____ days after Easter.
- Answer: 50

### CATEGORY 4: PRAYERS, SACRAMENTS & THE MASS

**$100** During Lent, the priest wears _____ vestments.
- Answer: Purple

**$200** A crucifix is different from a plain cross because it has a figure of _____ on it.
- Answer: Jesus

**$300** The _____ is the center and high point of the Mass.
- Answer: Eucharist

**$400** The Sign of the Cross is three things: a complete prayer, the start of another prayer, and a _____.
- Answer: Blessing

**$500** Before receiving Holy Communion, we pray: "Lord, I am not worthy that you should enter under my _____."
- Answer: Roof

### CATEGORY 5: LIVING AS DISCIPLES

**$100** A _____ is someone who follows Jesus.
- Answer: Disciple

**$200** During Lent we practice prayer, fasting, and _____.
- Answer: Almsgiving

**$300** How many Stations of the Cross are there?
- Answer: 14

**$400** One of the most common messages we learned this year is "Fear _____."
- Answer: Not

**$500** A vocation is a _____ from God to live our lives in a certain way.
- Answer: Call
