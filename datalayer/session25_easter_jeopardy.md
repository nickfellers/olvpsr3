### CATEGORY 1: CELEBRATING EASTER

**$100** Easter Sunday is the most _____ day of the Church year.
- Answer: Important

**$200** During the Easter season, the priest wears _____ or gold vestments.
- Answer: White

**$300** The Easter season begins with the Easter _____ on Holy Saturday night.
- Answer: Vigil

**$400** The last day of the Easter season is called _____.
- Answer: Pentecost

**$500** At Mass during Easter, before receiving Holy Communion, we pray to the _____ to have mercy on us.
- Answer: Lamb of God

---

### CATEGORY 2: GOD'S MERCY

**$100** Mercy is made up of compassion and _____.
- Answer: Forgiveness

**$200** In Psalm 103:8, we learn that God is slow to anger and abounding in _____.
- Answer: Mercy

**$300** Pope _____ declared a Year of Mercy in 2015.
- Answer: Francis

**$400** Jesus teaches us to give and not expect anything in _____.
- Answer: Return

**$500** Saint Paul wrote: "God, who is rich in mercy, brought us to _____ with Christ."
- Answer: Life

---

### CATEGORY 3: STATIONS & LENT

**$100** During Lent we practice prayer, fasting, and _____.
- Answer: Almsgiving

**$200** At the 14th Station, Jesus is laid in the _____.
- Answer: Tomb

**$300** The woman who wiped the face of Jesus was named _____.
- Answer: Veronica

**$400** Lent lasts for _____ days, the same number Jesus spent in the desert.
- Answer: 40

**$500** On Good Friday, we pray traditional ancient _____ — prayers asking God for help on behalf of others.
- Answer: Intercessions

---

### CATEGORY 4: THE RESURRECTION STORY

**$100** After Jesus died, a man named _____ placed his body in a tomb cut into rock.
- Answer: Joseph

**$200** The first person Jesus appeared to after rising from the dead was Mary _____.
- Answer: Magdalene

**$300** Jesus told the women at the tomb: "Do not be _____."
- Answer: Afraid (or sad)

**$400** _____ days after his Resurrection, Jesus ascended into heaven.
- Answer: 40

**$500** Paul wrote to the Corinthians: "Christ died for our sins... he was buried... he was raised on the _____ day."
- Answer: Third

---

### CATEGORY 5: THIS YEAR SO FAR

**$100** Carlo Acutis called the Eucharist his "_____ to heaven."
- Answer: Highway

**$200** God created us to be in community — Session 2 taught us we were "Created to Be _____."
- Answer: Together

**$300** Jesus fed 5,000 people with 5 loaves of bread and 2 _____.
- Answer: Fish

**$400** Jesus appeared to _____ with instructions to take care of his sheep — meaning his people.
- Answer: Peter

**$500** Jesus spoke to the Samaritan woman at the well to show that in Jesus there are no _____ or foreigners.
- Answer: Strangers

### FINAL JEOPARDY

**Category:** The Big Picture

**Clue:** At Easter we celebrate that God sent Jesus not because we were good — but to show us his _____ and _____.
