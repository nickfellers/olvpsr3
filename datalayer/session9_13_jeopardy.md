### CATEGORY 1: THE PASCHAL MYSTERY

**$100** The Paschal Mystery is the term that describes how Jesus _____ us.
- Answer: Saved

**$200** The Paschal Mystery includes Jesus' life, Death, Resurrection, and _____.
- Answer: Ascension

**$300** How many days after Jesus died did he rise from the dead?
- Answer: 3 (Three)

**$400** The word "paschal" comes from a Greek word meaning "to pass _____."
- Answer: Over

**$500** At Mass we pray: "We proclaim your Death, O Lord, and profess your _____ until you come again."
- Answer: Resurrection

---

### CATEGORY 2: THE MASS

**$100** How many ways is Jesus present at Mass?
- Answer: 4 (Four)

**$200** The _____ is the center and high point of the Mass.
- Answer: Eucharist

**$300** Name one gesture we do together at Mass. (hint: we go up and down)
- Answer: Standing, kneeling, or bowing

**$400** A blessed object that helps us grow in our spiritual life is called a _____.
- Answer: Sacramental

**$500** Before receiving Holy Communion, we pray: "Lord, I am not worthy that you should enter under my _____."
- Answer: Roof
