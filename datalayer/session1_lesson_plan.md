Unit 1\.  
This unit is all about Ignatian Spirituality. We’ll work through pages in book as time allows.

* **God is everywhere.** Ask the room to make a list of really neat places they’ve been. (God was there\! God made that\!)

* Focus on a personal feeling of God.

Optional:  
"St. Ignatius created a special bedtime prayer to help us remember where we saw God during our day. Let's try it\!"

Lead them through these simple steps:

1. **Thank You, God** \- "Think of one thing from today you want to thank God for"  
2. **God Moments** \- "When did you feel really happy or loved today? God was there\!"  
3. **Sorry Times** \- "Was there a time you weren't your best self? Tell God sorry"  
4. **Tomorrow** \- "Ask God to help you be kind and good tomorrow"

Practice this together with an example from a typical school day.

### **The "Finding God Everywhere" Game (5 minutes)**

Introduce the concept that God can be found in many places:

"Ignatius taught us to be like detectives looking for clues about God everywhere\! Let's practice\!"

Go through examples together:

* "When you help a friend who dropped their books, where is God?" (In your kindness\!)  
* "When you see a beautiful sunset, where is God?" (In the beauty\!)  
* "When your mom hugs you, where is God?" (In the love\!)

Have students pair up and think of their own examples to share with the class.

Note to self (from pre-read):  
**Discernment of spirits** is a key practice for making decisions. Ignatius developed methods for distinguishing between movements of the soul that lead toward God (consolation) and those that lead away (desolation). This involves paying attention to inner feelings and movements to understand what might be from God versus what might distract from spiritual growth.
