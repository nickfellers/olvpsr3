## LESSON 1: Discipleship — Following Jesus Today

**LESSON_ID:** G3-RE-001

### Source Reference
- Finding God Grade 3 Session 8: Jesus Gathers Disciples
- Scripture: Luke 5:1–11

### Core Objective
Students will understand that:
- A disciple follows Jesus.
- Following Jesus means trusting him and living like him.
- Discipleship applies to daily life (school, home, friendships).

### Theological Anchor
> "A disciple is someone who follows Jesus."

**Expanded:** Following Jesus means listening to him, trusting him, and acting like him.

### Time Breakdown (15 minutes)

#### 1. Opening Frame (2 min)

**Teacher Script:**
- "When Jesus said 'Follow me,' he didn't mean just walking behind him."
- "He meant listening, trusting, and living like him."

**Define:** A disciple = someone who follows Jesus.

#### 2. Pair Activity: Following Jesus Today (10 min)

**Instructions:**
"What would following Jesus look like today — at school, at home, or with friends?"

**Provide rails:**
- How we treat people
- What we do when things are hard
- How we help

**Students:**
- Discuss (3–4 minutes)
- Choose one example to share
- Teacher pulls 3–4 examples

**Affirmation line:** "That is what following Jesus looks like."

#### 3. Bridge Statement (3 min)

**Teacher says:**
- "Following Jesus is not always easy. Sometimes it feels scary. Sometimes we feel worried."

**Transition:**
- "Today we are going to meet someone who learned how to trust God instead of being afraid."

## LESSON 2: Saint Louise de Marillac — Fear Not

**LESSON_ID:** G3-RE-002

### Source Reference
- Finding God Grade 3 Session 23
- Scripture: Philippians 4:6–7

### Core Objective
Students will understand:
- Saint Louise experienced worry and uncertainty.
- She had a moment of trust while praying in church.
- Trust in God reduces fear.
- Trust leads to action and service.

### Scripture: Philippians 4:6–7

**Kid Version:**
> "Do not worry. Tell God everything. God will give you peace."

**Core takeaway phrase:**
> "Fear not. God is with you."

### Story Arc (10–12 minutes)

**Narrative Spine:**
1. Louise loved God.
2. Her life was difficult (loss, illness in family, uncertainty).
3. She felt worried and afraid.
4. While praying in church, she trusted God.
5. She realized she was not alone.
6. She began helping the poor and sick.
7. Trust led to action.

**Key theological sentence:**
> "God did not remove all her problems — but God removed her fear."

### Activity: From Worry to Trust (15 minutes)

**Materials:**
- Paper folded in half
- Crayons or pencils

#### Step 1 — Pair Discussion (3 min)
**Prompt:** "What does worry look like for a kid?"
**Boundary:** No personal sharing required.

#### Step 2 — Draw Worry (5 min)
**Left side label:** Worry
Students draw: Symbols, Faces, Situations

#### Step 3 — Trust Moment (2 min)
Teacher leads short prayer:
> "God, sometimes we feel afraid.
> Help us trust you.
> Help us know you are with us."

#### Step 4 — Draw Trust (5 min)
**Right side label:** Trust in God
**Prompt:** "Draw what it looks like when fear gets smaller and trust gets bigger."

**Examples (optional suggestions):**
- Light
- Calm heart
- Helping someone
- Feeling brave

---

## Integration Section (5 minutes)

**Teacher connects both lessons:**
- "Following Jesus means trusting God."
- "Trusting God means we do not have to be afraid."
- "Saint Louise followed God — and she learned to fear not."

**Students repeat:**
> "I can trust God. I do not have to be afraid."

---

## Master Thematic Thread: Discipleship = Trust

**Logical Flow:**
1. Jesus calls.
2. Disciples follow.
3. Following requires trust.
4. Trust replaces fear.
5. Trust produces service.

## Assessment Markers

Students demonstrate understanding if they can:
- Define disciple.
- Give one example of following Jesus today.
- Describe Saint Louise's turning point.
- Articulate that trust in God reduces fear.
- Connect trust to action.
