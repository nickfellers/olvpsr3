### CATEGORY 1: THIS YEAR SO FAR

**$100** The three Persons of the Holy Trinity are the Father, the Son, and the _____.
- Answer: Holy Spirit

**$200** Lent begins on _____ Wednesday.
- Answer: Ash

**$300** The three practices of Lent are prayer, fasting, and _____.
- Answer: Almsgiving

**$400** Jesus spent _____ days fasting in the desert. This is also the number of days in Lent.
- Answer: 40

**$500** In Session 9, we learned that the butterfly is a symbol of Jesus' _____ because it comes out of its chrysalis into new life.
- Answer: Resurrection

---

### CATEGORY 2: SAINTS & HEROES

**$100** Saint Louise de Marillac helped people who were poor and _____.
- Answer: Sick

**$200** Blessed Carlo Acutis was canonized — made a _____ — in April 2025. He is the first millennial to receive this title.
- Answer: Saint

**$300** God did not remove all of Saint Louise's problems — but God removed her _____.
- Answer: Fear

**$400** Carlo Acutis set his own _____ time limits because he didn't want anything to crowd out his love for God.
- Answer: Screen

**$500** Carlo Acutis said: "All people are born as originals, but many die as _____."
- Answer: Photocopies

---

### CATEGORY 3: PRAYERS & THE MASS

**$100** During Lent at Mass, the priest wears _____ vestments.
- Answer: Purple

**$200** Prayers asking God for help on behalf of another person are called _____.
- Answer: Intercessions

**$300** The Sign of the Cross is three things: a complete prayer, the start of another prayer, and a _____.
- Answer: Blessing

**$400** A crucifix is different from a plain cross because it has a figure of _____ on it.
- Answer: Jesus

**$500** Every time we pray the Apostles' _____, we proclaim our belief in the Paschal Mystery.
- Answer: Creed

---

### CATEGORY 4: STATIONS OF THE CROSS

**$100** How many Stations of the Cross are there?
- Answer: 14

**$200** At Station 5, soldiers forced a man named _____ of Cyrene to help carry the cross.
- Answer: Simon

**$300** At Station 6, a woman named _____ stepped through the crowd to wipe the face of Jesus.
- Answer: Veronica

**$400** How many times does Jesus fall under the weight of the cross during the Stations?
- Answer: 3 (Three) — at Stations 3, 7, and 9

**$500** Who condemned Jesus to death at the very first station?
- Answer: Pontius Pilate

---

### CATEGORY 5: CALLED TO RESPOND

**$100** A vocation is a _____ from God to live our lives in a certain way.
- Answer: Call

**$200** The Sacrament through which men become priests is called Holy _____.
- Answer: Orders

**$300** The Sacrament in which a man and woman agree to live in faithful love is called _____.
- Answer: Matrimony

**$400** The Holy Spirit calls us to share our gifts with the _____ and community.
- Answer: Church

**$500** Jesus said: "But many who are first will be last, and the _____ will be first."
- Answer: Last

**Category:** The Big Picture

**Clue:** This year we learned: Jesus saved us. He is with us at Mass. He tells us "Fear not." And he gave each of us gifts and talents to _____.
